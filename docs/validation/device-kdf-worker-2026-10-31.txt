ESP32-S3 PBKDF2 scheduling check
Date: 2026-10-31

The password verifier remains the PSA PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256 derivation at 100,000
iterations. It now runs in a low-priority worker pinned to the HTTP server's
core; the request task waits on a task notification. The worker shares core 1
with IDLE1, while higher-priority network tasks can preempt it.

On the ESP32-S3 normal firmware image, an authenticated USB-bridged login
returned HTTP 200. PBKDF2 took 30,533 ms. Seven periodic main-task HEALTH lines
arrived during the login, and captured serial output contained no task watchdog
warning or backtrace. The board was left on the normal firmware image. The
unchanged PBKDF2 known-answer self-test passed at boot, and the full pinned
verification script passed after the change.

This confirms the CPU1 idle-watchdog regression is addressed for the PBKDF2
operation. The calculation still has high latency. The six-second slow-body
concurrency and live-WebSocket scenario still needs to be rerun with a host that
can reach the device over its LAN; the current workstation cannot reach the
recorded station address. No physical probes or LCD were connected.
