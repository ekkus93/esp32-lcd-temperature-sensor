Connected ESP32-S3 isolated simulation qualification — 2026-10-30

Purpose: exercise the ESP32 HTTP, sensor registry, settings, accelerated history,
LittleFS, and memory/load paths without attaching physical probes or the LCD.

Built with pinned ESP-IDF v6.1 and the existing isolated configuration
(`CONFIG_MONITOR_SIMULATION=y`, physical `CONFIG_MONITOR_SENSOR_BUS` disabled).
The temporary application image was 1,998,736 bytes, SHA-256
`f505fceedc0fbd1bc5941de4a575c2657dce0df84681d93086eaf56e1275ccb6`. It was
written only to the app slot at `0x20000`; bootloader, partition table, NVS,
and history partition were not erased.

On-device tests passed:

- Five explicit fake providers, independent per-ROM calibration, labels,
  enable/disable, alert transitions, and settings across restart.
- Simulated LittleFS history query, downsampling, ROM filtering, unknown-boot
  isolation, bounded query rejection, and authenticated CSV export.
- Device preference validation, stale revision rejection, and persistence over
  restart in the isolated simulation namespace.
- Four authenticated WebSocket clients with the fifth rejected, followed by a
  120-second stalled-client test. The device closed the stalled socket, all
  120 concurrent authenticated HTTP checks succeeded (279 ms maximum), and
  30 health samples were captured while five simulated probes and accelerated
  history writes were active.

Load telemetry minima across the 30 samples: internal free heap 54,631 bytes;
largest internal block 31,744 bytes; cumulative internal minimum 20,384 bytes;
PSRAM free 8,346,508 bytes; cumulative PSRAM minimum 8,327,868 bytes; main-task
stack watermark 2,700 reported units. These measurements use simulated probes
and an initialized LVGL task with no physical panel attached.

The normal image was then restored by app-only flash at `0x20000` from
`firmware/build/temperature_monitor.bin` (2,001,568 bytes, SHA-256
`5b3e35634f799113b96ad550fc73cd1e52504b9ea0629f62fcbe97014b1cf261`). Final
20-second smoke passed with `simulated=false`, zero sensors, healthy LittleFS,
READY, Wi-Fi connected, and no panic. No LCD or physical probes were connected.

This validates firmware/software behavior on the real MCU with fake providers;
it is not physical probe, LCD, power-loss, long-soak, or release evidence.
