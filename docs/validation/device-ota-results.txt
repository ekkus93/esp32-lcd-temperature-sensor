Rollback exercise — 2026-10-30
  - Before staging, the device reported valid `ota_0`, connected station with
    saved credentials, and a healthy event journal. The oldest-event boundary
    and pagination cursor were saved privately for comparison.
  - The same image was uploaded to `ota_1`. About 2.3 seconds after HTTP 202,
    before two healthy acquisition cycles, `python -m esptool --chip esp32s3
    --port /dev/ttyACM0 --before default-reset --after hard-reset chip-id`
    reset the device. The boot log then loaded offset 0x20000 (`ota_0`),
    demonstrating rollback from the still-pending image.
  - The 15-second smoke passed with healthy history/event storage, READY,
    zero sensors, five-second health cadence and no panic. Authenticated API
    status reported valid `ota_0`; admin access and saved Wi-Fi reconnected.
    The event journal remained healthy with its prior oldest-event boundary
    and cursor intact.

This verifies bootloader rollback before image confirmation and preserves NVS
settings and the event-journal boundary. The aggregate history partition had no
sensor samples to compare, so preservation of populated temperature history
remains unverified. The LCD remained disconnected.
