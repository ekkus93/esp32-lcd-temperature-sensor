ESP32-S3 independent display refresh smoke
Date: 2026-10-31

The normal ESP-IDF v6.1 image with a dedicated 100 ms display-refresh task was
flashed to `/dev/ttyACM0`. The LCD and temperature probes were not connected.
The 15-second bare-board smoke passed: firmware reached READY, history and
event storage remained healthy, no fabricated probes were reported, and there
was no panic. HEALTH logs continued at approximately five-second intervals.
The sampled internal free heap was 75,419 bytes at the first health record;
minimum internal heap watermark was 42,644 bytes, largest internal block was
31,744 bytes, and minimum main-task stack watermark was 2,764 reported units.

This verifies build, startup, resource health and the independent task running
without crashing on bare board. It does not verify visible LCD refresh latency,
BOOT progress visibility, screen dwell, or physical probe continuity.

After the BOOT GPIO service was split into its own source module, the image was
rebuilt (`0x1ee060` bytes), reflashed, and the same 15-second smoke passed
again. The latest first-health sample reported 89,671 bytes internal free;
minimum internal heap watermark was 43,208 bytes, largest internal block was
31,744 bytes, and minimum main-task stack watermark was 2,764 units. No panic
or fabricated probe reading appeared. The API suite still needs re-enrollment
of the test-managed administrator before it can run on this image.

After adding stale-snapshot expiry/publication to the refresh task, a fresh
`0x1ee1a0` image was flashed and passed another 15-second bare-board smoke.
The latest health samples showed 74,711 bytes initial internal free, a 42,300
byte internal watermark, 31,744-byte largest internal block, and 2,668 units
minimum main-task stack. Storage remained healthy and no panic or fabricated
probe appeared. A stalled-acquisition/API freshness injection has not yet been
run.

After extracting the fake-clock snapshot-expiry policy into the production
`expire_display_snapshot` helper and adding an exact-threshold host regression,
the final image (`0x1ee5d0` bytes) was reflashed and passed a 15-second smoke.
Storage remained healthy; the first HEALTH record had 70,447 bytes internal
free, 43,184-byte internal minimum watermark, 31,744-byte largest block, and
2,668-unit main-task stack watermark. No panic or fabricated probe appeared.

The final retryable API-publication image (`0x1ee5f0` bytes) was flashed and
passed a 15-second bare-board smoke. HEALTH samples continued, with 70,315
bytes initial internal free, 42,256-byte internal minimum watermark, 31,744-
byte largest internal block, and 2,668-unit main-task stack watermark. History
remained healthy and no panic/fabricated probe was reported.

Repeat smoke after the board was reconnected on 2026-10-31 also passed using
the same `0x1ee5f0` image. The current run reported 80,687 bytes internal free
at first health, a 42,212-byte minimum watermark, a 31,744-byte largest block,
and a 2,668-unit main-task stack watermark. Firmware reached READY with healthy
history and event storage, zero sensors (bus disabled), and no panic. The LCD
and probes remain disconnected; visible refresh and sensor behavior are not
covered. Captured serial output: `.build/device-current-smoke.log` (local,
untracked build artifact).
